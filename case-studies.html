Revenue Engine

B2B technology consultancy, regulated sectors.

Entirely referral-dependent. Prior outbound attempts failed because targeting was wrong and messaging was generic.

Challenge
Entirely referral-dependent pipeline with failed prior outbound attempts.
What We Built
Four-layer ICP diagnostic across two verticals. Two parallel 7-touch email sequences. Two sending domains, six inboxes, 500+ verified contacts. Full compliance framework.
Result
Complete outbound infrastructure operational. Founder owns all domains, inboxes, contact lists, and documentation at close.

Methodology: The ICP diagnostic, not the email sequence, creates 80% of outreach success.

Challenge
Published clinical research, strong testimonials, but no system to turn expertise into market presence. A major conference was six weeks away.
What We Built
Voice OS from a single 78-minute extraction session producing 20+ content pieces. Four B2B outreach sequences. Pre-conference warmup and post-conference activation sequences. Content calendar across six platforms.

Challenge
