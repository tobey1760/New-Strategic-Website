The Origin

1760 Strategic AI was founded in honor of co-founders Matt and Mitch's mother, an entrepreneur who built something remarkable. She understood the craft of business: strategy, client relationships, execution. She did the work because that's what it took. That entrepreneurial spirit, that refusal to compromise on quality, is what 1760 is built on.

The Problem She Faced

Every founder knows the grind: the hours spent on manual processes, the work that scales but doesn't automate, the decisions that pile up because they're too important or too specific to delegate. Matt and Mitch watched their mother pour herself into that work, not because she wanted to, but because there was no other way. The methodology lived in her head. The systems didn't exist yet. AI didn't exist yet.

What We Build For

1760 Strategic AI exists to give founders what she didn't have: infrastructure that works the way they think. AI-powered tools that handle the manual hours so strategy and leadership aren't squeezed into the margins. We're not here to replace entrepreneurial judgment. We're here to multiply what founders can accomplish when their time isn't consumed by work that shouldn't require their attention. For anyone building something they believe in, we're the lever.
